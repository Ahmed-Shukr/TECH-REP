The weakest Odoo dashboards are the ones that start with a chart library. The strongest ones start with a meeting: what decision is this screen for, and what number would change that decision?

## Three layers

1. **Question** — occupancy this morning, revenue by category this month, sittings still unmarked.
2. **Aggregation** — a PostgreSQL-backed read that can answer the question without loading every record into the browser.
3. **Presentation** — OWL + Chart.js, KPI cards, and a path back to the source record.

If layer 2 is a client-side `searchRead` of thousands of rows, the dashboard will rot as soon as the database is real.

## KPI cards before charts

A manager can use a number. A chart is optional. Put the definition of the KPI next to the number: period, filter, inclusion rules. Two teams comparing "revenue" with different cancelled-state rules are not looking at the same product.

## Drill-through is not optional

A dashboard that cannot open the underlying records is a poster. In Odoo, the correct finish is an action that opens a list or form with the same domain the chart used.

## Chart.js is enough

For operational ERP dashboards, Chart.js covers time series, mix and comparison. The constraint is not the library. The constraint is whether the aggregation is honest and fast.

## What I refuse to do

I do not publish percentage-complete bars for skills, and I do not publish vanity dashboards for clients. If a widget does not change an operational decision, it does not belong on the home screen.
