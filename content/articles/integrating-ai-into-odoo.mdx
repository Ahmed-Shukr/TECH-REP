The useful pattern for AI in Odoo is assistance, not autonomy.

Classify a ticket. Retrieve a knowledge article. Draft a reply. Then stop. The agent accepts, edits or discards. The ERP remains the system of record.

## Why autonomy is the wrong default

ERP records have consequences: invoices, medical notes, exam marks, customer messages. A model that writes those records without a human gate is not "automation". It is an unreviewed user.

If you need automation, automate the deterministic parts: assignment rules, SLAs, reminders. Keep the model on the ambiguous parts: language, classification, first drafts.

## A clean integration shape

1. **Trigger** — ticket created or knowledge article requested
2. **Context pack** — the ticket fields plus retrieved articles, not the entire database
3. **Model call** — classify / retrieve / draft
4. **Proposal record** — stored next to the ticket
5. **Human action** — accept, edit, discard

Log the suggestion and the final text. You will need that when someone asks why a reply went out.

## Grounding

A suggested reply that cannot point at a knowledge-base record will invent policy. That is unacceptable in a hospital, a registrar or a school, and it is not much better in a generic helpdesk.

## What this looks like in the portfolio

The AI Helpdesk project is this pattern applied to Odoo support: classification and drafts under agent control. The interesting engineering is the boundary, not the model brand.
